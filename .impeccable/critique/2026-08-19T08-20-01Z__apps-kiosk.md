#### Design Specificity Verdict
**Verdict: Grounded, Dignified Community Keepsake System (PASS)**
GraceBooth successfully avoids generic SaaS marketing patterns (no gradient-mesh marketing cards, no fake metric counters, no crypto dark-mode tropes). The interface communicates reverence, warmth, and family/church event focus.

#### Deterministic & Technical Audit Score (17/20)

#### Priority Issues
- **[P1] Public Web Focus Ring Fails Non-Text Contrast (1.4.11)**: `--focus: #ffca4b` against white background yields only 1.57:1 contrast (WCAG AA requires 3:1).
  - *Fix*: Switch to `#0047ab` (8.2:1 contrast) or dual-ring.
  - *Suggested Command*: $impeccable harden apps/public
- **[P1] Missing Keyboard Focus Trap in Camera Setup Modal**: Tabbing inside Camera modal can escape to background operator buttons.
  - *Fix*: Add Tab focus trap matching PasscodeDialog.
  - *Suggested Command*: $impeccable harden apps/kiosk
- **[P1] Sub-11px Typography in Admin Badges and Mobile Day Stamp**: Status chips at 7px–9px violate legibility floor.
  - *Fix*: Elevate micro-copy to at least 11px with bold uppercase tracking.
  - *Suggested Command*: $impeccable typeset apps/kiosk
- **[P2] Blanket 0.01ms Reduced-Motion Overrides**: Destroys camera shutter visual feedback for guests with motion sensitivity.
  - *Fix*: Provide non-strobe opacity/border pulse alternative.
  - *Suggested Command*: $impeccable animate apps/kiosk
- **[P2] All-or-Nothing Photo Retake**: Review screen forces guests to discard all 4 photos when only 1 has closed eyes.
  - *Fix*: Provide single-shot retake option.
  - *Suggested Command*: $impeccable delight apps/kiosk
